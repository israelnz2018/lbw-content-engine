# Company Profile

- **Brand and positioning:** Educação pelo Trabalho

- **Website:** https://www.learningbyworking.com/

## What the company does

Learning by Working is a training and consulting company focused on data analysis, process improvement, continuous improvement, quality, and project management. The company uses Israel's professional knowledge together with an artificial-intelligence-enabled platform to make technical analysis and problem solving more practical and accessible.

## Products and services

- Training in Business Analytics, Lean Six Sigma, applied statistics, quality tools, and improvement project management.
- The GATE method: Gestão, Analisar, Técnica e Estatística.
- LBW statistical software for graphical and statistical analyses, with explanatory reports and AI-supported guidance.
- Consulting and support for process improvement, operational excellence, data analysis, and change management.
- Community, live sessions, mentoring, learning materials, and professional certifications.

## Target audience

- Professionals who work with quality, data, operations, business analysis, or process improvement.
- Managers and analysts who need to improve their technical and decision-making skills.
- People beginning their careers who want practical experience in problem solving and data analysis without programming.
- Independent consultants and professionals who want to offer training and analytical solutions under their own presence.

## Consultant platform model

LBW also provides the same platform structure to other consultants and partners. Each consultant can have a personalized address based on their name, replacing the current `israel` subdomain with the consultant's name, following a format such as `nomedoconsultor.edu...`. The consultant-facing experience may use their own identity and positioning while being supported by the LBW technology, methodology, and platform.

## Purpose and tone

The company's purpose is to make problem solving, data analysis, and continuous improvement simpler, practical, and useful for professionals and organizations. Communication should be clear, educational, practical, and approachable, avoiding unnecessary technical complexity and exaggerated promises.
